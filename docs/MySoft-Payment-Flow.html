From the website visit to a paid plan: how customers pay, and how the system and MySoft confirm the payment.

www.mysoftkh.com
attendance.mysoftkh.com

1Visit the website

www.mysoftkh.com → Features, Prices (by number of staff, branches included), FAQ, Telegram contact → Start free trial.

2Sign up

Company name, your name, phone, email, password, number of staff. The company and its admin account are created and the customer is signed in.

3Free trial

1–3 months (set by the super admin). All features. A reminder banner shows 7 days before the trial ends.

4Choose a plan · Billing & plan

Staff range (and its branches), Monthly or Yearly (months free). An invoice is created, e.g. INV-2026-01001, status Unpaid. The new period starts after the current one ends, so no days are lost.

How does the customer pay?
A · ABA PayWay (online)Automatic · seconds
Customer

Pay online · ABA PayWay

The system sends a signed checkout (invoice, amount) to ABA PayWay's secure page.

Customer on ABA PayWay

Pays with KHQR, ABA Pay or card

KHQR works from ABA, ACLEDA or any Cambodian bank app.

System

Confirms with ABA PayWay

PayWay notifies the server; the server asks PayWay directly: approved, full amount, USD. The notice alone is never trusted.

✓ Approved

Invoice Paid at once. Customer returns to Billing: "Payment received".

✗ Not paid

Declined, cancelled or less money: stays Unpaid; pay again.

B · Bank transfer (ABA / ACLEDA)Checked by MySoft
Customer

Pay by bank transfer

Sees MySoft's account name, number and KHQR, the exact amount, and the remark to write: the invoice number.

Customer

Transfers, then uploads the receipt

Screenshot or PDF from the bank app (max 5 MB). Invoice: Receipt sent · checking.

Super admin (MySoft)

Telegram alert → Platform › Payments

Opens the receipt and compares it with the ABA / ACLEDA statement (amount, remark, date).

✓ Approve

Invoice Paid. Usually within one working day.

✗ Reject

With a reason the customer sees; they can send the receipt again.

C · Cash or otherRecorded by MySoft
Customer

Pays MySoft directly

Cash at the office, another bank, or an agreed deal.

Super admin (MySoft)

Subscriptions › Record payment

Plan, 1 or 12 months, amount received, note. Paid immediately.

Also by the super admin

Subscriptions › Adjust

Longer trial, extra time, other plan, custom branch limit, or Free plan.

Invoice PAID

Plan extended: "paid until" moves forward by 1 or 12 months, starting after the current trial / paid period.

Limits from the plan: number of active staff and branches; everything keeps working.

Everyone is told: Telegram alert to MySoft; the invoice shows Paid in the customer's Billing & plan.

Payment rules, statuses and responsibilities

What happens when a payment is late, how an invoice moves between statuses, upgrades, and who does what.

www.mysoftkh.com

If the customer does not pay automatic

Free trial / paid periodEverything works. Banner 7 days before the end (trial) or 5 days (paid).
7 grace daysEverything still works; "Payment due" warnings.
Read-onlyAdmins can sign in, view, export and pay. Staff cannot scan; nothing can be changed.
startend dateend date + 7 days

As soon as an invoice is Paid (any method), the company is active again: no data is lost. The grace length is set in Plans & settings.

Invoice statuses

Unpaid Receipt sent · checking Paid ✓ Rejected Cancelled receipt uploaded super admin approves ABA PayWay confirmed · or recorded by the super admin rejected uploads again cancel or new invoice

Upgrades and plan changes

During the trial: the customer changes the plan freely (it must fit their active staff and branches).
During a paid period, bigger plan: an upgrade invoice for the price difference × the months left; the plan changes as soon as it is paid, the end date stays.
Smaller plan: chosen when renewing; refused while the company has more active staff or branches than it allows.

MySoft set-up checklist Platform › Plans & settings

  • Prices for each staff range, and branches per plan (1 · 2 · 10 · unlimited).
  • Free trial 1–3 months, grace days (7), free months when paying yearly (2).
  • Bank accounts: ABA and ACLEDA account name, number and KHQR image.
  • ABA PayWay: merchant ID and API key from ABA (paywaysales@ababank.com); give ABA the pushback URL https://attendance.mysoftkh.com/api/public/payway/callback; test in sandbox, then turn sandbox off.
  • Telegram alerts: bot token and chat ID, to hear about every sign-up, receipt and payment.
  • Website contact: phone, Telegram (@mysoftkh) and email.

Security

PayWay requests are signed with MySoft's secret API key (HMAC-SHA512), and every payment is confirmed with ABA PayWay before a plan is extended. Receipts are stored privately (only the customer and the super admin can open them). Every payment, approval and change is written to the audit log.
